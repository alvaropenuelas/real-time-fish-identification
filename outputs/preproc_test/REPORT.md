## STEP 1a — aspect ratio is NOT the cause

The pipeline (`src/realtime.py`, lines 51 / 234) passes the **raw frame** straight to
`detector(...)` / `detector.track(...)` with no `cv2.resize` or crop. Ultralytics
letterboxes internally to 640 with aspect preserved. There is no offending manual-resize
line.

Confirmatory test (letterbox vs deliberately squashed 640×640) over 8 sampled frames:

| path | total boxes (8 frames) |
|---|---:|
| letterbox (what the pipeline uses) | 28 |
| squashed square (aspect destroyed) | 12 |

Squashing *reduces* detections, it does not inflate them. So aspect-ratio distortion is
not generating the ghosts. **Not a preprocessing bug.**

## STEP 1b — what the ghost boxes land on

Inspecting saved key frames at conf 0.25:

- frame 0415: boxes sit on **pale anemone tentacles (0.42), empty bluewater / floating
  particles (0.43), coral/anemone structure (0.73)** — alongside correct boxes on real
  clownfish.
- frame 0249: boxes land on anemone-heavy regions; the prominent central clownfish is only
  loosely caught at low conf.

Ghosts land on **background reef structures (anemone, coral, rock) and water particles**,
not on deformed/squashed fish. This is a **domain gap**: the DeepFish training data is open
seagrass/marine footage, not a reef aquarium full of anemones, coral and backscatter.

## STEP 3 — root cause + recommendation (NOT auto-picked)

**Root cause: domain gap, not a preprocessing bug.** Ghosts are real false positives on
background reef structures and particles the detector never saw in DeepFish training.

**No threshold is baked into any config.** A higher conf cleans the clip but visibly costs
real detections (clarkii gone at 0.8), and the test-recall cost cannot be quantified here —
so recommending a value now would violate the rule that the threshold is decided on the
test set. **Decision deferred to you, pending the test-set val.**

**The real fix is not a higher threshold.** Because this is a domain gap, the durable fix is
adding **hard-negative background frames** (anemone / coral / particle-only crops with empty
labels) to detector training, so the model learns those structures are not fish. A
confidence bump is at best a stopgap that trades recall for fewer ghosts.
